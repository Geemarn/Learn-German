## Die Foto-Hörgeschichte: "Ellas Glückstag"

Since there is no text provided in your book for the introductory audio story, here is a complete breakdown of what happens in **"Ellas Glückstag" (Ella's Lucky Day)**, along with the vocabulary and expressions used in the audio!

### 1. Plot Summary (Was passiert?)
**Ella Wegmann** is a journalist for the newspaper *"Stadt-Kurier"*. She has her own series called *"Ellas Tag"*. 
Her colleague, **Sami**, calls her at 2:00 PM. He urgently needs an interview on the topic of **"Glück" (Luck/Happiness)** by 6:00 PM today. Ella is very annoyed because she actually had private plans for the afternoon and she can't do magic (*"Ich kann doch nicht zaubern!"*).

While waiting at the bus stop, a friendly older woman named **Hillie** recognizes Ella from the newspaper. Hillie makes a quick phone call to her friend, **Manfred Schulze**, and sends Ella to his house for the interview. 

When Ella arrives at Mr. Schulze's house, she notices an old desk lamp. She mentions that she actually wanted to go to the flea market today to buy a new desk lamp because hers broke. Mr. Schulze gives it to her as a gift! Then they do the interview about a time Mr. Schulze was very lucky (he played the lottery). 

In the end, Ella calls it a true **"Glückstag"** (Lucky day) because she got:
1. A great tip from a nice woman.
2. An interesting interview partner.
3. A new desk lamp.
4. And she gave Sami 10 minutes of anxiety!

### 2. Audio Vocabulary & Spoken German (Gesprochenes Deutsch)

When listening to this audio, you will hear a lot of natural, colloquial German. Here are the most important phrases and what they mean:

#### Expressing Annoyance / Surprise (Umgangssprache - Slang)
*   **"Sag mal: Geht’s noch, Sami!?"** - *Are you crazy, Sami? / Have you lost your mind?* (Very common when someone asks for too much).
*   **"Der spinnt ja wohl!"** - *He must be crazy!*
*   **"Du regst mich auf, Mann!"** - *You are getting on my nerves / making me angry, man!*
*   **"Ich kann doch nicht zaubern!"** - *I can't do magic!* (Meaning: I can't just make an interview partner appear out of nowhere).
*   **"Nö, das mach' ich nicht."** - *Nope, I'm not doing that.* ("Nö" is colloquial for "Nein").

#### Important Nouns
*   **die Journalistin** - journalist (female)
*   **das Interview** - interview
*   **der Notfall** - emergency (Ella says: *"Das ist kein Notfall, das ist ein Witz!"* = That's not an emergency, that's a joke!)
*   **auf die Schnelle** - quickly / in a hurry
*   **der Flohmarkt** - flea market
*   **die Schreibtischlampe** - desk lamp
*   **das Glückserlebnis** - lucky experience

#### Important Verbs
*   **aus/machen (hat ausgemacht)** - to agree on / to arrange (*"Das hatten wir gestern ausgemacht."* = We agreed on that yesterday.)
*   **sich erinnern an (+ Akk)** - to remember (*"Erinnerst du dich?"* = Do you remember?)
*   **stören** - to disturb (*"Ich hoffe, ich störe Sie nicht."* = I hope I am not disturbing you.)
*   **kaputt/gehen (ist kaputtgegangen)** - to break

### 3. Grammar Focus of Chapter 1
The audio story also introduces the two main grammar topics of Chapter 1:

**1. The Simple Past Tense (Präteritum)**
When Mr. Schulze tells his story about the lottery, he uses the written/storytelling past tense.
*   *spielen* -> er **spielte** (he played)
*   *gewinnen* -> er **gewann** (he won)
*   *kommen* -> er **kam** (he came)

**2. Sentences with "als" (When...)**
In B1, you learn to use **"als"** for an event that happened *once* in the past. It pushes the verb to the end of the sentence!
*   **Als** ich noch Lotto gespielt **habe**, ... *(When I still played the lottery, ...)*
*   **Als** ich ins Spielcasino gegangen **bin**, ... *(When I went to the casino, ...)*
